Recorded locally on every device: waves, purchases, AFK returns, play time.

Start: one Legionary with Shield Bash (key 1). Each wave has 1–5 enemies, at most two at a time. After the wave 5 boss, upgrade the town to unlock the tower: Archer (single target, Volley) or Catapult (area).

Every hero has a spell: 1 — Front (Legionary: Shield Bash, Hoplite: Hold the Line), 2 — Tower (Archer: Volley), 3 — Wall (Priestess: Blessing). Tap the spell button or the hero. U opens upgrades, Esc closes.

Rotation. When a slot has a substitute, heroes get tired: −15% damage per wave in a row after the first. A hero who sat out a wave comes back rested: +25% damage and the spell ready. Upgrades belong to the slot, so swapping is free.

Each wave starts at full HP for free. The farmer produces food; 3 patrol kills give 1 gold. Speed 1× / 2× / 100× affects combat and production. No saves or offline income yet.
